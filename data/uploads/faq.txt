STORE FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

1. What shipping methods are available?
We offer Standard (7-14 business days) and Express (3-5 business days) shipping. All orders are processed within 24 hours.

2. Do you ship internationally?
Yes, we deliver to over 50 countries. Shipping costs and delivery times will be calculated at checkout based on your location.

3. How can I track my package?
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an email with a tracking link and a unique tracking number.

4. What is your return policy?
You can return any unused item in its original packaging within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.

5. How do I initiate a return?
Please visit our Returns Portal or contact our support team with your order number to receive a return authorization.

6. Who pays for return shipping?
Return shipping is free for defective items or incorrect orders. For change-of-mind returns, the customer is responsible for shipping costs.

7. What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX), PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

8. Is it safe to use my credit card on your site?
Yes, our website uses industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption to protect your financial data during the transaction.

9. Can I cancel my order?
You can cancel your order within 1 hour of placement. After that, the order may have already been processed for shipping.

10. Do I need to create an account to shop?
No, guest checkout is always available. However, having an account lets you save your address and view order history.

11. What if my item arrives damaged?
If your order is damaged during transit, please contact us within 48 hours with photos of the package for a priority replacement.

12. Are there any items that cannot be returned?
For hygiene reasons, some items like earrings, swimwear, and intimate apparel cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

13. How do I apply a discount code?
You can enter your promo code at the Checkout page in the Discount Code field before finalizing your payment.

14. Do you offer gift wrapping?
Yes, you can add gift wrapping and a personalized note to your order for an additional $5.00 during checkout.

15. How can I contact customer support?
Our team is available 24/7 via the Live Chat button on our website or by emailing support@fictionalstore.com.
