# Clothing Customer Support FAQ

1. Question: How do I know which size will fit me best?
   Answer: Every product page features a Size Guide link with specific measurements in centimeters and inches to help you find the perfect fit.

2. Question: Do your sizes follow US, UK, or EU standards?
   Answer: We provide a conversion chart on each page, showing international equivalents for US, UK, and European sizing.

3. Question: Will the clothes shrink after the first wash?
   Answer: Most of our cotton items are pre-shrunk, but we recommend washing at 30°C and avoiding high-heat drying to maintain the original shape.

4. Question: Do you offer a try-on service upon delivery?
   Answer: Yes, if you choose our branded courier delivery, you have 15 minutes to try on your items before finalizing the payment.

5. Question: What materials are used in your activewear collection?
   Answer: Our activewear is made from a high-performance blend of recycled polyester and elastane for moisture-wicking and flexibility.

6. Question: Are the colors of the clothes exactly as they appear on the website?
   Answer: We strive for color accuracy, but slight variations may occur due to different monitor settings and studio lighting.

7. Question: How should I care for my silk or wool garments?
   Answer: We recommend professional dry cleaning or careful hand-washing with specialized detergents for all delicate silk and wool items.

8. Question: Do you have a physical store where I can try things on?
   Answer: Yes, we have several flagship stores in major cities. You can find the nearest location using the Store Locator on our website.

9. Question: Can I modify my order after it has been placed?
   Answer: Orders can be modified or cancelled within 30 minutes of placement. After that, they are sent to our warehouse for processing.

10. Question: Do you offer alterations or tailoring services?
    Answer: We provide basic hemming and minor adjustments at our physical retail locations for a small additional fee.

11. Question: Is your denim raw or pre-washed?
    Answer: Most of our denim is pre-washed for comfort, but we have a "Raw Heritage" line for enthusiasts who prefer unwashed denim.

12. Question: What is the "Sustainable Choice" label on some products?
    Answer: This label indicates that the garment is made from at least 50% organic, recycled, or sustainably sourced materials.

13. Question: Do you restock items that are sold out?
    Answer: Many of our core items are restocked regularly. You can sign up for "Back in Stock" notifications on the product page.

14. Question: Are your shoes available in wide or narrow widths?
    Answer: Most of our footwear comes in a standard width, but specific models are available in Wide (E) fits, as noted in the description.

15. Question: How do I remove a stain from a delicate fabric?
    Answer: We suggest blotting the stain immediately with cold water and consulting a professional cleaner for best results.

16. Question: Can I purchase a gift card for your clothing store?
    Answer: Yes, we offer both digital e-gift cards and physical gift cards that can be used online or in-store.

17. Question: Do you sell maternity or plus-size clothing?
    Answer: Yes, we have dedicated Maternity and Plus-Size collections designed for comfort and style.

18. Question: Is the winter outerwear rated for specific temperatures?
    Answer: Yes, our coats and parkas include a "Warmth Rating" to help you choose the right gear for your local climate.

19. Question: Why is my size out of stock in some colors but not others?
    Answer: Inventory varies by color and style based on demand. Popular seasonal colors often sell out faster than neutrals.

20. Question: Do you offer student or military discounts?
    Answer: Yes, we offer a 10% discount for students and military personnel through our verification partner.

21. Question: Are your leather products genuine or synthetic?
    Answer: We offer both genuine top-grain leather and high-quality vegan leather options, clearly labeled in the product details.

22. Question: Can I return an item if I removed the tags but didn't wear it?
    Answer: For a full refund, items must be returned with all original tags attached and in their original packaging.

23. Question: How often do you release new collections?
    Answer: We release major seasonal collections four times a year, with smaller "capsule" drops every month.

24. Question: Do you have a loyalty program for frequent shoppers?
    Answer: Yes, our "Style Points" program rewards you for every purchase, giving you early access to sales and exclusive discounts.

25. Question: What should I do if the item I received is defective?
    Answer: Please contact our support team with a photo of the defect within 48 hours of delivery for a free replacement or refund.
